title: Employee Onboarding Guide

New employees complete a structured onboarding program during their first two weeks. Day one includes account provisioning, workstation setup, and an introduction to company tools including email, chat, and the internal knowledge base. During the first week, new hires attend sessions covering company culture, organizational structure, and role-specific expectations set by their manager. The second week focuses on hands-on training with the tools relevant to their team, paired with a designated mentor for guidance. HR schedules a 30-day check-in to address any open questions and collect feedback on the onboarding experience. Employees are expected to complete all mandatory compliance training modules, including data protection and workplace conduct, within the first 30 days. Onboarding completion is tracked in the HR system.
